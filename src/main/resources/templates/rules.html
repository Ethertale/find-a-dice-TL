⚖ The Code of Conduct

⚔

By using Find A Dice you agree to abide by the following rules. Read them, know them, keep the realm civil.

📜 General Rules

I
Minimum Age

You must be 13 or older (or your country's minimum age for online services) to register and use this platform.

II
No Harassment or Hate Speech

Attacks or derogatory content based on race, gender, religion, sexuality, disability, or any other personal characteristic are strictly forbidden.

III
No Swearing or Explicit Language

Keep your language appropriate in all areas - chats, posts, campaign notes, character bios, and group content.

IV
No Impersonation

Do not impersonate other users, platform staff, or real people and public figures.

V
No NSFW Content

All images, descriptions, and text - including character bios, campaign descriptions, and group posts - must remain appropriate. Sexual and gory content is not permitted.

VI
No Spam or Advertising

Unrelated links, repeated posts, and self-promotion outside of intended features (such as the future Marketplace) are not allowed.

VII
No Cheating or Exploiting Bugs

Intentionally abusing bugs - such as dice roller manipulation or unauthorized access to campaigns or chats - is forbidden. If you find a bug, report it instead.

VIII
Respect Account Boundaries

Do not share accounts or attempt to access another user's account by any means.

IX
Use the Reporting System

If you witness a rule violation, use the Feedback/Report tool. Do not take matters into your own hands.

X
No Accountability for User Conduct

We do not mediate personal disputes, scams, or misrepresentations between users - including campaign scheduling conflicts or marketplace deals gone wrong. Reports help us moderate; they do not resolve disputes on your behalf.

⚙ Feature Rules

⚔
Campaigns

Dungeon Masters are responsible for their campaign's content - notes, chat, and uploaded maps. Inappropriate DM notes or maps remain subject to the General Rules even if only the DM can see them.

📄
Character Sheets

Sheets must reflect actual campaign use. Intentionally entering false or broken data to grief a DM or party members may be reported and reviewed.

💬
Chat & Campaign Chat

All chats are treated as public for moderation purposes, even when between specific participants. Abuse can be reported and reviewed by staff.

👥
Groups

Guildmasters (group creators) are responsible for moderating their own group's posts to a reasonable extent. Repeated abuse within a group may result in the group being reviewed or removed.

🎲
Dice Roller

The dice roller is for casual and flavour use only. It is not a verified fair-roll system and must not be used for competitive or real-money purposes.

🖼
Image URLs

All image links - for heroes, campaigns, and groups - must point to appropriate, non-infringing content. Do not link to copyrighted material you do not have the rights to use.

🛒
Marketplace (Coming Soon)

No counterfeit or illegal goods. Transactions occur entirely at users' own risk. We are not a payment processor or escrow service.

These rules are subject to change as the platform grows. Continued use of Find A Dice constitutes acceptance of the current rules.
