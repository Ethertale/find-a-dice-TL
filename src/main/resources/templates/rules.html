GENERAL RULES

Minimum age - must be 13+ (or your country's minimum age for online services) to register.

No harassment or hate speech - no attacks based on race, gender, religion, sexuality, disability, etc.

No swearing or explicit language - in chats, posts, campaign notes, character bios, group content.

No impersonation - of other users, staff, or real people/public figures.

No NSFW content - images, descriptions, or text (character bios, campaign descriptions, group posts) must stay appropriate; no sexual or gory content.

No spam or advertising - unrelated links, repeated posts, self-promotion outside intended features (e.g. Marketplace once it exists).

No cheating/exploiting bugs - intentionally abusing bugs (e.g. dice roller manipulation, unauthorized access to campaigns/chats) instead of reporting them.

Respect account boundaries - no sharing accounts, no attempting to access others' accounts.

Reporting - use the Feedback/Report tool for rule violations rather than taking matters into your own hands.

No accountability from us for user conduct - we don't mediate personal disputes, scams, or misrepresentations between users (e.g. campaign scheduling conflicts, marketplace deals gone wrong). Reports help us moderate, not resolve disputes on your behalf.


MORE RULES

Campaigns - DMs are responsible for their campaign's content (notes, chat, uploaded maps). Inappropriate DM notes/maps are still subject to General Rules even though only the DM sees them.

Character Sheets - must reflect actual campaign use; sheets with intentionally false/broken data to grief a DM or party may be reported.

Chat & Campaign Chat - treated like public chat for moderation purposes even though messages are between specific participants; abuse can be reported and reviewed by staff (see DeletedReport/FeedbackReport system).

Groups - Guildmasters (creators) are responsible for moderating their own group's posts to a reasonable extent; repeated abuse in a group may lead to the group itself being reviewed/removed.

Marketplace (future) - no counterfeit or illegal goods; transactions happen at users' own risk, we are not a payment processor or escrow service.

Dice Roller - for casual/flavor use only; it's not a verified fair-roll system for competitive or real-money purposes.

Image URLs (hero, campaign, group images) — must be links to appropriate, non-infringing images; no copyrighted material you don't have rights to.
